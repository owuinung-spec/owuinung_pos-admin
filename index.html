Promotions

Offers, campaigns, referral rewards and notices to groups of shops.

Offer codes

Give a code to a lead. When you record their payment (Customers, then Extend), type the code and it adjusts the amount or adds free days.

Campaigns

A campaign groups a promotion with dates and a goal, so you can see what it brought in. Tag leads with a campaign in the lead form; codes can be tied to one here.

Referral rewards

When a shop brings another shop, record it here. Once the new shop has paid, give the referrer free days with one click.

Notice to a group of shops

Shows in the dashboard of every shop in the group, as your Notice does for one shop. It stays until cleared, so clear it when the offer ends.

Message templates

Used for every one-tap WhatsApp button in the admin. Fill-ins: {name} {business} {owner} {town} {date} {link} {price}. You still press send in WhatsApp. Send to people you know or who asked about Owi; WhatsApp can ban numbers that message strangers in bulk.

Reminders: renewals and approvals

Customers expiring within 7 days, expired in the last 30 days, or waiting for approval. Each button opens WhatsApp with the message filled in. You still press send.

App settings

Update the app for your customers and show an announcement in it.

App updates

The app checks this when a user opens the dashboard. Below the minimum build number, the app is blocked until they update. Below the latest, it only shows a small "new version" banner. Leave minimum at 0 to never block. Raise the build number (versionCode in the app) with every release.

Announcement to every customer's app

Shows as a blue banner on the dashboard of every business. Users can dismiss it for the session.

Firebase Realtime Database (all shop data and photos)

Firebase does not let a web page read its own usage meter, so this is an estimate: it counts the records of every business and measures a small sample of each kind. Treat it as accurate to roughly ±25%. The exact figure is always on the Firebase usage page.
Open exact usage in Firebase
Press Measure now. It downloads a small sample from every business (usually a few MB) and counts against the free monthly download allowance, so use it when you need it, not all day.

Projection against the free Spark limits

Free limits for Realtime Database (from Firebase pricing): 1 GB stored, 10 GB downloaded a month, 100 phones connected at once. This looks forward from today's estimate using how fast your shops add bills, so press Measure now first. It is a model with assumptions you can change, not a promise.
